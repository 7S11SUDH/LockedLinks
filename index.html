How to play

Find 4 groups of 4 words that share something in common. Pick 4 tiles and press Submit. You can make 4 mistakes.

The twist: 9 of the 16 words are locked. Tap a locked tile to play a quick mini-game. Win it and the word is revealed.

Lose the mini-game and the tile stays locked for good. You'll have to work out which group it belongs to without seeing it. You can also put a locked tile into a guess without ever playing its game.
